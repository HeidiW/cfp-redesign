For trade partners

Work with us, not against us.

Painters, drywall contractors, tile setters and general contractors who need decorative plaster on a job they are already running. And finishing companies who want the systems we built for ourselves. Three ways in.

01

Subcontract to us

You hold the contract, we do the plaster. We show up insured, on schedule, with our own material and our own crew, and we do not talk to your client about anything except the wall.

Most common on restaurant and hotel buildouts where one room needs a finish nobody on site can do.

02

White-label supervision

You have the crew but not the specialist. We review the substrate, set the sequence, train your people on the finish, and stand behind the result — under your name if you prefer.

Priced as a day rate. Cheaper than a callback.

03

Run your shop on JobsOxo

The platform we built to run this business — inquiry, estimating, scheduling, invoicing, payment, payroll and reconciliation in one place. Sold on its own, or set up with us alongside.
