About Conor

A trade learned by hand, run like a business.

Conor Foy has been finishing walls in New York since 2002. The work is done on site by a small crew who have been together a long time, with material mixed to the room rather than ordered off a shelf.

What changed over twenty-three years is everything around the trowel. Estimating, scheduling, daily logs, invoicing, payroll — we rebuilt all of it, then built the software to run it. Architects and general contractors now hire us as much for the way a job is managed as for the finish at the end.

If you are drawing a room and want to know what a surface can actually do, that conversation is free, and we enjoy having it.
